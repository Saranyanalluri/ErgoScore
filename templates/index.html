CURRENT SCOPE

Designed for practical video-based screening

ErgoScore uses 2D pose estimation from a single camera view. Frames with insufficient landmark visibility may be excluded from scoring. When lower-body landmarks are unavailable, a neutral knee-angle assumption is used.
